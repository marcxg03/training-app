## Weekly split

| Day | Session | Type |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Plyos + Track → **Upper** | lifting (+ conditioning) |
| Tue | Cardio (AM) → **Lower** (Power + Strength) | lifting (+ cardio) |
| Wed | **Basketball** (1–2 h) | recovery/sport |
| Thu | Cardio (AM) → **Push** | lifting (+ cardio) |
| Fri | **Pull** + Basketball | lifting (+ sport) |
| Sat | **HYROX** (half or full) | conditioning |
| Sun | **ATG Recovery** + cardio | recovery |

Default rep target: compound **6–8**, isolation **7–10 / 8–10**, all **2 working sets** unless noted.

---

## MONDAY — UPPER
**Conditioning first — Plyos + Track:** choose 4 plyo exercises, 2 × 10 m each (bank: Broad Jumps, Pogo Hops, Bounds, …) + **4 × 400 m sprints**.

**LIFT** (3 rounds; *note: "starting with back feels good"*):
- **Round 1**
  - Back — Vertical Pull Down · 2 × 6–8
  - Chest — Upper/Mid Chest (bank) · 2 × 6–8
  - Shoulders — Overhead Press · 2 × 6–8
- **Round 2**
  - Back — Horizontal Row · 2 × 6–8
  - Chest — Upper/Mid Chest (bank) · 2 × 6–8
  - Shoulders — Lateral Raise · 2 × 6–8
- **Round 3** (*weak points + arms*)
  - Lower Chest / Triceps — Dips · 2 × 7–10
  - Cable SA Rear Delt Fly · 2 × 7–10
  - Biceps · 2 × 7–10

## TUESDAY — LOWER (Power + Strength)
**Cardio (AM):** Speed 3.0, Incline 10, 45 min–1 hr (*do in morning*).

**LIFT**
- Deadlift — bank: BB/DB RDL · Sumo DL · 2 × 4–6
- Squat — BB Back Squat (ATG; heel-elevated if needed) · 2 × 4–6
- Walking Lunges · 2 × 20
- Back / Glute Extension · 2 × 6–8
- Calf Raise · 2 × 8–10
- Abs / Core — choose 2 (bank: Hanging Leg Raise, Dragon, Decline Bench Curl, L-Sit Crunch, Planks, Woodchopper, Pallof Press) · 2 × 8–10 each

## WEDNESDAY — BASKETBALL
1–2 hours. (Recovery/sport session — no lift.)

## THURSDAY — PUSH
**Cardio (AM):** Speed 3.0, Incline 10, 45 min–1 hr (*do in morning*).

**LIFT** (warm-up: open/mobilize shoulders, activate back/chest)
- Bench — bank: BB Bench Press · BB Incline Smith Press · 2 × 6–8
- Shoulder Press — bank: BB Overhead Press · DB · Machine · 2 × 6–8
- Dips — Bodyweight or Weighted · **2 × failure**
- Lateral Raise — DB or Cable · 2 × 7–10
- Chest Fly Machine · 2 × 6–8
- Triceps — bank: Cable Pushdown (SA or both) · Overhead Extension · V-bar · Rope

## FRIDAY — PULL + BASKETBALL
**LIFT**
- Vertical Pull — Lat Pulldown (neutral/wide) · 2 × 6–8
- Horizontal Pull — Chest-Supported; bank: DB Row · BB Row · 2 × 6–8
- SA Cable Kneeling Pulldown *or* Pullover (DB or Cable) · 2 × 6–8
- Face Pull · 2 × 8–10
- Rear Delts — bank: Rear Delt Fly Machine · SA Cable Rear Delt Fly · 2 × 8–10
- Biceps — choose 1–2 (depending on fatigue) · 2 × 10

**Then:** Basketball ~1–2 hours.

## SATURDAY — HYROX
Half **or** full:
- **Half** = choose 4 stations; **4 × 1 km + station** each round.
- **Full** = all 8 stations + **1 km per** station.

## SUNDAY — RECOVERY
**Full ATG Recovery** · 2 × 7–10 (bank/circuit): Dead Hang · DB Pullover · ATG Shoulders (External Rotation, TYI Raise) · ATG Split Squat · Good Morning · Jefferson Curl.
**Cardio:** Speed 3.0, Incline 10.0, 45 min–1 hr.
**Optional:** Sauna / Cold Plunge if desired.
